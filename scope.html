Scope of Work

Six work packages from data collection to implementation

The work is organized as a clear sequence so each technical stage can feed the next.

01

Data collection and review

AMC records, rainfall data, drainage and sewerage assets, ongoing works, and stakeholder inputs.

02

GIS database development

An integrated spatial platform covering networks, stations, STPs, drains, lakes, and natural channels.

03

Demand forecasting

Population, wastewater, runoff, land-use growth, and climate-aware design storm projections to 2057.

04

Hydraulic modelling

Sewer and storm water network simulation, bottleneck identification, and flood-risk assessment.

05

Master plan preparation

Augmentation strategy, trunk sewer planning, STP expansion, and nature-based drainage measures.

06

Implementation planning

Phasing, prioritization, and a practical roadmap for AMC.
