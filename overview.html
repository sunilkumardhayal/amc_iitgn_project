Project Overview

Why this master plan matters

Ahmedabad is expanding rapidly. This project builds an integrated framework to assess existing sewerage and storm water systems, forecast future demand, identify weak points, and shape a phased roadmap up to 2057.

Integrated Planning

One public hub for the whole project

Splitting the content into pages makes the site easier to navigate and more suitable for project updates, downloads, and future expansion.

Technical Workflow

Data, modelling, and implementation

The workflow combines GIS database development, hydraulic and hydrologic modelling, population and runoff forecasting, master plan preparation, and implementation planning.

Designed For

Clearer access and professional presentation

The structure is optimized for stakeholders who need quick access to the project story, the outputs, and the next steps.

Outcome

Better sequencing and better decisions

The final deliverable is not just a report. It is a practical roadmap for AMC that supports investment planning, phasing, and resilient infrastructure priorities.
