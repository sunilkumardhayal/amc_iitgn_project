Project Overview

Planning context for a rapidly growing metropolitan system

Ahmedabad is expanding across core urban areas and growth corridors. A 30-year drainage and sewerage roadmap is needed to align climate resilience, flood mitigation, wastewater management, and capital planning.

Spatial Data & GIS Framework

Digitize, standardize, and organize the city network

High-resolution elevation data, catchment delineation, asset registries, and drainage and sewerage layers are brought into a consistent geodatabase for analysis and decision support.

Hydrodynamic & Hydraulic Simulation

Assess network capacity under future rainfall and growth

City-scale computational modelling will test runoff response, sewer conveyance, pumping constraints, and overflow risk under peak rainfall and urbanization scenarios.

Strategic Phasing & Policy

Translate analysis into 5-year capital blocks

Prioritization will be organized into actionable capital investment phases that balance risk reduction, service reliability, and expansion pressure.
