Scope of Work

Six work packages for a structured 30-year planning program

The program is sequenced so each stage builds directly into the next, from baseline data consolidation to the final phased capital roadmap.

WP 1

Inception, Baseline Data Collection & Spatial Geodatabase Creation

Review of existing master plans, compilation of AMC asset registries, ground validation, and spatial layer harmonization in GIS.

WP 2

Hydrological Assessment & Stormwater Catchment Analysis

Analysis of historical precipitation intensity-duration-frequency curves, runoff modelling, and identification of chronic waterlogging hotspots.

WP 3

Hydraulic & Sewerage Network Assessment

Assessment of existing trunk mains, pumping stations, and sewage treatment plant capacities against projected population loading.

WP 4

City-Scale Hydrodynamic Modelling & Gap Analysis

Integrated 1D/2D hydraulic simulations to identify bottleneck zones under varying climate and urbanization scenarios.

WP 5

Master Plan Formulation & Infrastructure Intervention Design

Recommendations for network augmentation, decentralized retention and recharge basins, nature-based drainage solutions, and STP upgrades.

WP 6

Phased Capital Investment, Asset Management & Final Roadmap

30-year phased roadmap from 2027 to 2057 with institutional capacity building, standard operating procedures, and a sequenced implementation program.
