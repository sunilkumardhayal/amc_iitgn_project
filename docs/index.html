Planning Ahmedabad’s Next 30 Years: A Resilient City-Scale Drainage and Sewerage Master Plan (2027-2057)

Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation and IIT Gandhinagar are developing a long-horizon master plan to modernize stormwater and wastewater infrastructure through spatial databases, hydraulic simulation, phased capital planning, and implementation-ready technical outputs.
