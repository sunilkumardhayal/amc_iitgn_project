Delivery Plan

12-month schedule with milestone-linked outputs

The timeline keeps the work readable for public stakeholders while still showing the technical flow.

Month 1

Inception and data collection

Inception report, work plan, data request list, and stakeholder-gap assessment.

Months 2-3

GIS database development

Spatial database v1 with network layers, stations, STPs, watersheds, and land-use mapping.

Months 4-5

Demand forecasting

Population, wastewater, runoff projections, and demand forecasting report.

Months 6-8

Hydraulic and hydrologic modelling

Calibrated models, surcharge analysis, flooding assessment, and validation report.

Months 9-12

Master plan, roadmap, final handover

Draft plan, final report, GIS files, models, and AMC presentation.
